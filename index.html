All Songs

1. Adding Songs & Videos

Click Import Files in the bottom-left menu to pick .mp3 or .mp4 files from your device (videos play as audio-only). You can also click Add MP3/MP4 via URL to stream a song using a direct web link.

2. Playing, Searching & Shortcuts

Click any song row to start playing. Use the top search bar to find songs by title or artist instantly. At the bottom bar, use Shuffle, Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Loop (loop all or loop 1 song), and the volume slider. Keyboard shortcuts: press Spacebar to pause/play, and Left / Right Arrows to skip backward or forward 5 seconds.

3. Liking, Editing & Deleting Songs

Click the Heart icon on any song (or on the bottom player) to save it to Liked Songs. Click the Pen icon on a song to rename its title, change the artist name, or update its audio link. Click the Trash icon to delete a song from your library.

4. Playlists, Song Order & Covers

Click the + button next to Playlists in the sidebar to create a new playlist. Click the Folder+ icon on any song to add or remove it from your playlists. Drag and drop songs up or down (or click the small up/down arrows) to change the play order. Inside a playlist, you can click Customize Cover (pick a color theme or upload your own picture), Rename, or Delete.

5. Synced Lyrics & Custom Colors

Click Studio in the bottom-right corner and open the Lyrics tab. Click .VTT to upload a lyric file or Type to write lyrics yourself (prefix lines with [00:15] for live sync). Use the color bar at the top of the Lyrics panel to customize your highlight and text colors.

6. Sound Equalizer & Ambient Mixer

Inside the Studio panel, open 5-Band EQ to boost bass, sharpen vocals, or choose a ready-made sound preset. Open the Ambient tab to mix relaxing background sounds—Soft Rainfall, Vinyl Crackle, or Brown Noise—right over your songs.

7. Playback Speed & Sleep Timer

In the bottom-right bar, click 1.0x to speed up (1.25x, 1.5x) or slow down (0.85x) your music. Click the Moon icon to set an automatic Sleep Timer (15, 30, or 60 minutes) that pauses your music when time runs out.

8. Listening Stats & Private Cloud Sync

Click Detailed Stats in the sidebar to see your weekly listening hours, total hours played, daily average, 7-day activity chart, top songs, and top artists. Click Log In / Sign Up to sync your listening durations, play counts, and playlists across devices—while keeping your song titles private on your own device.
